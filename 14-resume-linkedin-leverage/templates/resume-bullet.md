## LinkedIn Post Templates

### Style 1: The Story Post

```
Last weekend, I built something that solved a problem I face every day.

As a student, I waste hours each week looking for available study spaces
on campus. I walk into rooms — occupied. Check the library — full.
End up in a noisy cafe wondering "what if someone just SHOWED me where
to go?"

So I built StudySpot. 📚

It uses real-time occupancy data from building sensors to show available
rooms on a live campus map. Open the app, see green pins, walk there.

The result? Average search time dropped from 14 minutes to 3.

Built with Next.js, PostgreSQL, and a lot of caffeine at [Hackathon Name].

#hackathon #webdev #students #nextjs
```

## Hashtag Research Method

Before posting, spend 5 minutes finding the right hashtags:

1. **Search LinkedIn for your hackathon's name.** Find 2-3 posts that got 100+ reactions. Copy their hashtags.
2. **Search for your tech stack.** "#nextjs" has 50K+ posts. "#reactjs" has 200K+. Use both.
3. **Check trending hashtags.** If your project relates to AI, "#artificialintelligence" or "#generativeai" are relevant.
4. **Add 2-3 niche hashtags.** "#hackathonproject" (3K posts), "#builtwithlove" (1K posts), "#studentdeveloper" (2K posts).
5. **Keep total hashtags between 3-8.** More than 8 looks spammy on LinkedIn.

**Pro tip:** Put hashtags in the first comment, not the post body. LinkedIn's algorithm penalizes hashtag-heavy posts.

## Engagement Optimization Tips

- **Post within 48 hours of the hackathon.** Momentum matters. The story is fresh, your emotions are real.
- **Tag your teammates.** They'll reshare, doubling your reach.
- **Include a demo GIF or video.** Posts with video get 3x more engagement on LinkedIn.
- **Ask a question at the end.** "What's your biggest hackathon lesson?" drives comments.
- **Reply to every comment within 24 hours.** The algorithm rewards conversation.
- **Share to relevant LinkedIn Groups.** "Hackathon Enthusiasts," your university's alumni group, your tech stack's community group.
- **Cross-post a shorter version to Twitter/X** with a link to the full LinkedIn post. Different audiences, same content.
- **Send a DM to 3-5 people** you think would genuinely find it interesting. Not spam — genuine outreach.

## Resume Formatting Notes

- **Use bullet points, not paragraphs.** Recruiters scan, they don't read.
- **Quantify everything possible.** "Improved load time" → "Improved load time by 2.3 seconds (60% faster)."
- **Put the tech stack in the bullet.** It helps with ATS keyword matching.
- **Order bullets by impact, not chronology.** Lead with your strongest result.
- **One hackathon = one bullet (usually).** Unless you won or it's genuinely exceptional, one line is enough.
- **Include the hackathon name.** "Built at HackMIT 2024" adds credibility.
